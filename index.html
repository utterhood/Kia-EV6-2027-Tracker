Daily comparison and EV6 lifecycle definitions

New Today and Dropped Today compare the latest complete snapshot with the final complete snapshot on the previous observed Eastern calendar day. Today means the snapshot date in America/New_York, not your device's date. Same-day reruns keep the same prior-day baseline. Missing dates are not filled, so a comparison can span more than one day. With no earlier observed day, both counts are zero baseline counts. New includes returning VINs. Dropped means absent from the latest complete feed, not a confirmed sale. EV6 lifecycle confirmation is unchanged: a VIN becomes confirmed missing after two complete missing runs, and a return then becomes a reappearance.

First seen is an observation date, not a build date. A sticker's dealer assignment does not prove physical arrival. Estimates require sufficient 2027 history.

VIN Production Tracking

Observed serial bounds are not production totals. Sequence coverage is not market share, and unobserved serials do not establish actual builds or their destinations. Numbers below the first observed 2027 serial are not proven gaps. Factory and model-year sequences are kept separate.

Historical VIN Tracking

Monthly highs are cumulative observed serial maxima within each model-year and factory sequence, not monthly production. First seen is an observation date, not a manufacturing date. No new observations carry the prior maximum forward. A missing prior month is insufficient history.
